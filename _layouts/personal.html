About

I am a master's student in Artificial Intelligence at Beijing Institute of Technology and an Agent R&D Intern at Tencent Cloud, where I contribute to Octop, an open-source agent platform. My work connects agent systems engineering with research on long-term memory and embodied planning.

I am interested in how agents maintain useful context, retrieve relevant experience, and turn observations into decisions and actions over extended interactions. In particular, I explore how these capabilities can support agents in the physical world, connecting perception, spatial memory, and planning to act under uncertainty and adapt to feedback.

A question that drives my work

How can agents remember what matters and use it to act over long horizons?
